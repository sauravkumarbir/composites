What does COMPOSITES mean?

English (American / British):
Composites = materials made by combining two or more different materials (for example carbon fiber + plastic). The result is stronger, lighter, or has better properties than the individual materials alone. Also means “made up of different parts”.

Composites in different languages (one by one)

1. English (American): Composites / Composite materials
